✈️ Travel & Airport FAQ Knowledge Base

## 🛂 Common Visa Types
1. Tourist Visa
- Purpose: Short-term leisure, sightseeing, visiting friends/family.
- Validity: Usually 30–90 days.
- Key Docs: Passport, travel itinerary, proof of funds, accommodation booking.

2. Student Visa
- Purpose: Study abroad.
- Validity: Duration of study program.
- Key Docs: Admission letter, financial proof, medical insurance.

3. Work Visa
- Purpose: Employment in a foreign country.
- Validity: Varies (months to years, tied to employment).
- Key Docs: Job offer/contract, employer sponsorship.

4. Transit Visa
- Purpose: Short stay at airport/city while connecting flights.
- Validity: A few hours to a few days.
- Key Docs: Confirmed onward ticket.

5. Schengen Visa (Europe)
- Covers: 27 European countries (France, Germany, Italy, etc.).
- Validity: Short stay up to 90 days in 180 days.
- Special Note: Apply at the embassy of the country you’ll stay longest.

6. US Visa (B1/B2, F1, H1B)
- B1/B2: Business/Tourist.
- F1: Student visa.
- H1B: Work visa (specialty occupations).

## 🛄 Airport & Flight FAQ
1. What is check-in?
- The process of confirming your ticket, receiving a boarding pass, and dropping off luggage.
- Options: Online (24–48 hrs before), or at airport counters/kiosks.

2. What is carry-on baggage?
- Small bag allowed inside the cabin.
- Typical rule: 1 personal item + 1 cabin bag (7–10 kg).

3. What is checked baggage?
- Luggage stored in aircraft hold.
- Weight limit: Usually 20–30 kg depending on airline.

4. What is a boarding pass?
- A document (paper or digital) issued after check-in.
- Includes: Gate number, seat, boarding time.

5. What does "layover" mean?
- A stop at an intermediate airport between flights.
- Short layover: 1–5 hours.
- Long layover: 6+ hours, sometimes overnight.

6. What is a direct vs. non-stop flight?
- Non-stop: Flies from departure to destination with no stops.
- Direct: May stop for refueling/boarding but you don’t change planes.

7. Do I always need a visa for transit?
- Depends on the airport and nationality.
- Example: Some countries require a Transit Visa even if you stay airside.

8. What are duty-free shops?
- Stores at airports selling tax-free goods (alcohol, perfumes, electronics).

9. What documents are needed for international travel?
- Passport (valid 6+ months), visa (if required), flight ticket, accommodation proof.

10. How early should I arrive at the airport?
- Domestic flights: 1.5–2 hours before departure.
- International flights: 3 hours before departure.
